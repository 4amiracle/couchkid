mission.

couch kid exists to help people feel like themselves. we believe emotional vulnerability shouldn't be rare or radical — it should be accessible. through conversations with experts in psychology, mental health, and human experience, we sit with the feelings most people are afraid to name. we invite therapists, researchers, and people who've been through it to tell us what they know — not the polished version. the honest one. couch kid is not a brand that talks at you. it's a conversation that sits with you.
